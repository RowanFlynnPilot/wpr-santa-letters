Letters to Santa 2026

Your name on Wausau’s holiday mailbag

Every December, kids across the Wausau area write to Santa and Wausau Pilot & Review publishes their letters. This year the mailbag is a page of its own: families send letters, read everyone else’s, post their child’s to Facebook and print a reply from Santa. One business presents all of it.
