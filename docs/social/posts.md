# Letters to Santa: what to post, and when

Made by `scripts/browser-tests.sh social` from `kit.json`; change that file and run it again rather than editing this one.
Each post has a feed picture (1080 by 1350, for Facebook and Instagram) and a story (1080 by 1920). Every post names the
presenting sponsors, as the sponsor sheet promises.

## Santa’s mailbag is open

When: Nov. 27, the day letters open.
Pictures: `1-letters-open-feed.png`, `1-letters-open-story.png`.

> Santa’s mailbag is open! Parents and guardians, send your child’s letter to Santa through Dec. 18: type it, email it or mail it. We post letters as they come in and deliver every one to the North Pole on Dec. 23. https://wausaupilotandreview.com/letters-to-santa/
>
> Letters to Santa is presented by Jones & Jones, Maher Water, One Community Bank and PGA.

Picture description (alt text): An airmail envelope on a snowy night reads “Santa’s mailbag is open.” Send your child’s letter through Dec. 18. We deliver them all on Dec. 23. Presented by Jones & Jones, Maher Water, One Community Bank and PGA.
