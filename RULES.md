# Letters to Santa: rules (draft for Shereen)

A starting point for the rules page the form links to (`VITE_RULES_URL`). Bracketed parts are decisions to make. It describes how the tool actually works, so if a rule changes, the code may need to change too (CLAUDE.md, Open decisions). Have whoever reviews WPR's contest rules look at it before it goes up.

---

**Letters to Santa 2026** is a holiday feature from Wausau Pilot & Review, a nonprofit newsroom, [presented by SPONSOR].

**Who can write.** Children 17 and under. A parent or legal guardian sends the letter for the child [and the child must live in ____].

**How to send a letter.** From 12 a.m. Nov. 27 through 11:59 p.m. Dec. 18, 2026, Central time:
- on the form at wausaupilotandreview.com/letters-to-santa,
- by email to editor@wausaupilotandreview.com, or
- by mail to Santa, care of Wausau Pilot & Review, PO Box 117, Wausau, WI 54402. Mailed letters must arrive by Dec. 18.

Include the child's first name and age, the town if you'd like it published with the letter, and a way to reach a parent (an email address, or a return address on a mailed letter). One letter per child.

**What we publish.** The letter, a photo of it if you send one, and the child's first name, age and town, on our website, in our newsletters and on social media. We never publish last names, addresses or a parent's contact details. An editor reads every letter before it's posted. We keep each child's spelling, and we may take out a last name, a street address, a school or anything else that identifies a child, or remove a photo. We may decline any letter. All the letters run together on Dec. 23.

**The prize drawing.** No purchase or donation is necessary. Every letter received by Dec. 18 that goes in the mailbag is entered. Each letter is one entry, so a family with two children who each write has two entries, but no family can win more than one prize; we know a family by the email address or the return address its letters came with. Letters with no way to reach a parent, and letters from the households of Wausau Pilot & Review staff, can't win. Winners are drawn at random after Dec. 18, announced Dec. 23 in the mailbag by first name, age and town (if given), and contacted by email or at the return address. A winner who doesn't reply within 7 days forfeits the prize, and it's drawn again among families that haven't won, if any remain. Odds depend on the number of letters. Prizes are provided by our sponsors and can't be exchanged for cash.

**Your information.** We use a parent's name and contact details only to reach you about the letter and any prize, and to send our newsletter if you ask for it. We never sell or publish them, and we delete them from our Letters to Santa records on Jan. 6, 2027, two weeks after the letters are delivered; the database's daily backups that still hold them expire within a week after that. The published letters stay up. [If staff use it: To help type in letters that come by mail, we may have an AI tool, Anthropic's Claude, read a photo of the letter; an editor checks every word against the letter itself. Anthropic doesn't use what we send it to train its models.]

**Taking a letter down.** Email editor@wausaupilotandreview.com anytime, and we'll take it off our website, the Dec. 23 story included. A newsletter that has already gone out can't be recalled. A letter that has won a prize gives up the prize when it comes down.
